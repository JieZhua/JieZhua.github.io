About

Welcome to my website. My name is Zhu Huajie.

I am an architecture student at XJTU and POLIMI, with a minor in computer science. I work as a designer, developer, and thinker: designing spatial narratives, building digital workflows with Python and computer vision, and writing about cities, images, and everyday experience.

01

I am aDesigner

I design through proportion, light, structure, and atmosphere, moving from classical spatial research to measured drawings, models, digital reconstruction, and portfolio narratives.

02

I am aDeveloper

I use Python, C, algorithms, data structures, and computer vision as tools for thinking, automation, and building clearer links between spatial information and digital workflows.

03

I am aThinker

I observe how rooms, streets, images, and systems shape movement and memory, turning architectural thinking into notes on cities, visual culture, and everyday experience.
